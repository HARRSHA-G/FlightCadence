About FLIGHT CADENCE.

Evolution of India's #1 Trip Support Company.

FLIGHT CADENCE has evolved from being just an ATC Flight Planning Company to India's #1 Trip Support company in just 2 years!! Founded in 2015 by Pravahya Consulting Pvt Ltd with a vision to create an Indian company with global solutions plus reduce flight planning costs.

Solving Industry Challenges

Indian Charter Aviation market is unregulated with a lot of uncertainties be it knowing Ground Handling rates, Star Hotel room rates, Fuel & Doctor availability, or Crew Transportation. Every delay previously required multiple phone calls starting from ATC.
